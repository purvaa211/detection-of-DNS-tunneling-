Two-Sample Hypothesis Testing (Welch's t-Test)

Testing whether mean values between Normal and Suspicious traffic differ significantly
Formal Statistical Hypotheses:
• Null Hypothesis (H₀): μ_normal = μ_suspicious (The population means of the two traffic categories are equal).
• Alternative Hypothesis (H₁): μ_normal ≠ μ_suspicious (The population means are significantly different).

Type I Error (False Positive, α)

Rejecting H₀ when H₀ is actually true (e.g. falsely classifying legitimate high-volume browsing as tunneling). Bound at α = 0.05.

Type II Error (False Negative, β)

Failing to reject H₀ when a true difference exists (e.g. failing to detect low-and-slow tunneling payloads).
